# Permanent project direction

The human owner abandoned the iPad/iOS build on 25 October 2026. Do not resume
iOS development, iPad packaging, simulator work, signing or physical-iPad QA.
Only a new explicit instruction from the human owner can reverse this decision.
Older iPad artifacts and evidence are historical archives, not an active gate.

The continuing targets are the native macOS desktop wrapper and the browser
game served by the Ubuntu/private-LAN backend. The desktop wrapper uses
SwiftUI/AppKit and WebKit/Ruffle, separate from the abandoned iOS Rust player.

Read README.md and TAKEOVER.md before changing code. Preserve authored prices,
timers, identities, imported fields, save transaction boundaries, God Barn
snapshots, feature refusals and all sixteen supported worlds. Enchanted Glen and Holiday Lights are dropped; do not expose their offers or travel. Reactivation requires new human authorization. Use unique synthetic data, cookies and
unused ports. Only one client may write a farm. Verify an owned process before
stopping it; never reuse a recorded PID.

Keep private game inputs, credentials/cookies, saves, screenshots, evidence,
compiled runtimes and build products out of Git. Do not import old workspace
history. SOURCE_MANIFEST.json seals the initial source snapshot; later changes
need reviewed diffs and updated tests/acceptance, not a relabeled old PASS.

Do not claim native window/gameplay, Ubuntu execution, long-session stability
or deployment from unit tests or a build alone. Keep PASS, FAIL and UNRUN
separate. Browser graphics and native-window checks are distinct. Historical eighteen-world receipts remain historical. Retired-world UI defects are outside the active release; unrecovered active-world systems keep their documented limits.
No automatic push or deployment. Mediterranean and later Wave 2 restoration
remain continuation work, separate from this stability handoff.

This is an explicitly authorized clean-start release. No existing farms are carried forward. The next Bifrost installer must wipe its clearly identified managed old save/snapshot/history/authentication state. Do not add migration, backup promotion or old-farm recovery work. Ship no old player state. Wipes must refuse unrelated/system/source paths. Preparation does not authorize live deployment. Test persistence/restart with new synthetic farms.
