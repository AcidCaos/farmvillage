# World readiness — sixteen-world clean-start release

**This table records testing with prepared resources.** A fresh source clone does not yet have automatic resource installation; see [current development status](HANDOVER_STATUS.md) and [resource setup](docs/RESOURCE_SETUP.md).

**Bounded acceptance handoff, 25 October 2026.** All sixteen browser scenes/HUDs passed, and twelve native Mac crop loops passed. The Mac then locked: four remaining native loops and the final fresh post-restart visual checks are UNRUN. This table distinguishes ordinary playable scope from unfinished regional systems and platform gates.

This is a clean-start release: no previous farms, snapshots, sign-in state or player history is carried forward. QA starts with new synthetic farms.

The supported targets are the native macOS SwiftUI/AppKit application with WebKit/Ruffle and the browser game on Ubuntu/Linux. **iPad/iOS is abandoned.** Actual Ubuntu-host execution has not been available in this sweep; tests of the Linux hosting adapter on macOS do not establish an Ubuntu deployment pass.

## What players can reasonably expect

- **Five strongest playable alpha worlds:** Home/October, English Countryside, Sunflower Meadows, Lighthouse Cove and Fields of El Dorado. These have the deepest recorded ordinary and representative special-system checks. None is a complete recreation of every historical feature.
- **Eleven playable in the documented basic mode:** Mediterranean Riviera, Fairytale Fields, Sweet Acres, Oasis Gardens, Emerald Valley, Toy Town, Horseman's Hollow, Australia, Mystical Groves, Mistletoe Lane and Atlantis. They have actual ordinary farming/decorating/storage/travel/reload acceptance, not just background screenshots. Their regional production, landmarks, treasures, scores and growth still require substantial restoration or checking. Mistletoe has an additional, separately checked Patisserie slice.

**Dropped by the owner:** Enchanted Glen and Holiday Lights are excluded from this release. They are not part of the playable roster or this sixteen-world acceptance.

October is a Home creation preset, not an additional world. A missing or intentionally refused feature is recorded separately from a reproduced defect. An accepted representative does not verify every recipe, stage, occupant or imported variant.
